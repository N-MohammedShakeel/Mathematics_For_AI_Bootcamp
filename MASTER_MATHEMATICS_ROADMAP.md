# 🧭 Complete Mathematics for AI/ML Master Curriculum Roadmap

Welcome to the **Mathematics for AI/ML Bootcamp**! This repository is a comprehensive, hands-on, and rigorous curriculum designed to take you from foundational mathematics to the cutting-edge mathematical concepts powering modern Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Generative AI (Transformers & LLMs), Diffusion Models, and Reinforcement Learning.

---

## 🗺️ Curriculum Overview & Module Navigation

```
Mathematics for AI/ML Bootcamp
├── 0. Introduction
├── Basics Of Algebra (High School Pre-requisites)
├── 1. Linear Algebra (Vectors, Matrices, SVD, PCA, Tensors & Einsum)
├── 2. Statistics and Probability (EDA, Distributions, Inference & Hypothesis Testing)
├── 3. Calculus (Single-Variable, Multivariable, Jacobians, Hessians & Integrals)
├── 4. Information Theory and Parameter Estimation (Entropy, Cross-Entropy, KL/JS Divergence, MLE & MAP)
├── 5. Optimization Theory (Convexity, KKT, AdamW, Loss Landscapes)
├── 6. Stochastic Processes and Sampling (Markov Chains, MDPs, Monte Carlo & MCMC)
├── 7. Discrete Mathematics and Graph Theory for AI (Graph Laplacians, GNNs & LLM Decoding)
└── Applications (Linear/Multiple Regression, PCA, Deep Learning Derivatives)
```

  - Linear Algebra (17 Einsum Attention) $\to$ Information Theory (01-05) $\to$ Stochastic Processes (01-02 MCMC & Diffusion intuition) $\to$ Discrete Math (01-02 GNNs & LLM Sampling).
